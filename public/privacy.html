Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

What Seeker Pulse does

Seeker Pulse is an independent tool that reads public blockchain and dApp catalog information to provide activity estimates and dApp discovery. It is not an official Solana Mobile service.

Information processed

When you submit a .skr domain or wallet-related lookup, the service processes the information needed to resolve the identity and retrieve public activity data. dApp searches process your search message and catalog query.

The service may receive standard technical information from hosting and performance services, such as request timing, browser information, and error information.

What we do not request

Seeker Pulse does not ask for private keys, seed phrases, wallet signatures, transaction approvals, or payment credentials. The dApp finder does not execute transactions.

Third-party services

The service may use Helius for blockchain data, PostgreSQL/Prisma for catalog storage, Vercel for hosting and performance measurement, and an optional server-side AI provider for recommendation explanations. Their own policies may apply.

Retention and security

Do not submit secrets or personal information in chat messages. We take reasonable steps to protect server-side data and credentials, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project creator through the project’s public profile.
